## Top 3 insights
1. **Action + pride abroad wins.** Indian forces doing real things alongside other nations (drills, flags side by side) beat every announcement.
2. **A "first" or a motto makes the hook.** "First of class" and "Victory is our sole aim" carried the commissioning post.
3. **Transformation arcs and Agniveers perform.** "Blueprint to battle ready" was the best reel; Agniveer posts outperformed most news posts.
