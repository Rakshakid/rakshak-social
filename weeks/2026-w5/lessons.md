## Top 2 posts
1. Reel "Operation Cactus" (3 Nov): 320 views, 278 reach, 6.1 s avg watch, 40% view rate (best of the week); FB 205 views. Hook: a date and one hard fact ("3 November 1988 ... about 9 hours later, Indian paratroopers were on the ground"), a real operation, three services, one mission. Segment: veterans. CTA: veterans register early. Matches the W5 competitor scan: real action and national pride beat soft stories.
2. Carousel "Fauji brats, your Diwali came with a posting order" (2 Nov): 213 views, 90 reach, 11 interactions (most of the week). Hook: "Fauji brats only" names the segment; shared nostalgia (cantonment mela, 40 "cousins"). CTA: a question ("Which posting gave you your best Diwali?") + tag a friend, then the fauji brats voucher offer.

## Lessons for the Strategist
1. Lead with a hard fact or date in the first 2 seconds and line 1. Operation Cactus (date + "9 hours") held viewers twice as long as the softer illustrative stories (6.1 s vs 3.0-4.1 s). Prefer real operations and real dates; keep illustrative stories but open them on a concrete number or moment.
2. Shares are zero for the second week running, and every W5 caption still opened with the "🎁 first 500 ... vouchers" line. Move the offer to the end card and caption close; open on the story. Let the W6 Trial Reel A/B (offer-first vs story-first) confirm it.
3. A comment question that names the segment works: the brats carousel had the most interactions. Add one such question to every carousel.
