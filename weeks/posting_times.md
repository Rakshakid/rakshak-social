## Conclusion
- The data supports the founder's 7:00 PM order. The one finding with some weight: reels at 9 AM did worse than reels at 7:30 PM (median reach 92 vs 247). This is still weak (n=4 vs 6, content differs).
- There is not enough data to set a different time for each weekday. Most weekdays have one or two posts.
- Carousels show no time effect.
- Content drives far more of the spread than time: the two biggest reels (1,547 and 1,051) were a strong story and an Agniveer piece, not a time slot.
