## Lessons for the Strategist
1. Reach fell ~75% and shares fell to zero in the first week where every reel and caption opened with the "🎁 first 500 ... vouchers" offer. One week is not proof, but the posts read as ads. Test it this week: keep the gift tag small, open caption line 1 and the first 2 seconds on the story hook (as W3's winners did), and run that as the Trial Reel A/B (offer-first vs story-first opening).
2. Evening beat morning: the 19:36 Navy reel out-reached all three 09:00 reels. Use the data-based posting time (TEAM.md posting-time order) and do not default reels to 09:00.
3. Keep the Rakshak Nari, proud-payoff stories: both top posts were fauji-wife stories with a positive turn. Put the Navy reel on YouTube (it was missed) and give every Short a hook title.
