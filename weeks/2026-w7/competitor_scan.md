## Top 3 insights
1. **An invitation beats an announcement.** The marathon post asked civilians to run with the Navy and gave a date and a link. It topped every new item, ahead of exercises with five navies.
2. **A story told in chapters keeps people coming back.** Arrive → harbour → sea: three Busan posts each got 16–18K, and the last chapter was the best. Each post ended on a hook for the next.
3. **Numbers need a face.** 22,000 nautical miles with nobody in frame got about 5K. "Three stranded persons rescued" and "largest-ever Agniveer batch" moved people. The press told the Sudarshini voyage through one child on the jetty.
