## Top 3 insights
1. **A historic "first", in one plain line, beats everything.** "Landed… on its maiden attempt" doubled the next best post with a two-sentence caption.
2. **Forces serving people, with real numbers, is the second engine.** Villages, people stranded and kilos per lift made the HADR post the most shareable story of the fortnight.
3. **A quiz with a reward drives comments; promos die.** The word challenge got about 10x the usual comment ratio; seminar and event reels were the weakest items.
