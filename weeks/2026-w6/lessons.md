## Lessons for the Strategist
1. Scene + real person + news peg + triumph is the winning formula: the Agniveer reel held viewers 2.5x longer than the softer illustrative stories (7.8 s vs 3.1 s), confirming W5's Operation Cactus lesson (hard fact / date first). Open every reel on a concrete time and place in the first 2 seconds, tie it to a dated event, and end on a win. Agniveers are a founder priority theme: build on this segment while the first batch is completing its four years (verify current news first).
2. Photo carousels reach less than half what reels reach (97 vs 230 IG views) and earn no shares. Build every item as a reel (W7 already is), and get carousel-to-reel swaps approved before the post date or drop the carousel format.
3. Shares are still near zero (1 in two weeks). The share line sits only in the caption; consider a segment-named share ask the founder can approve ("Send this to an Agniveer you know") as the caption's closing line.
