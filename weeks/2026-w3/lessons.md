## Lessons for the Strategist
1. Proud, one-segment hooks win reach: both top reels opened on a triumphant line naming who it is for. Pity/struggle carousels (Veer Nari "starts from zero") got the lowest views and no shares. Keep every hook positive and segment-named.
2. Watch time collapses after the hook (4.4 s on the top reel). The Rakshak Nari reel held 29.6 s, so story holds people once they stay. Put the payoff/offer in the first 3 s (gift tag from frame 1) and test the opening via this week's Trial Reel A/B pair.
3. Facebook and YouTube are near-dead (0 shares, 0 subs). Give Shorts a hook title (not the caption) and pin the offer as the first comment on FB.
