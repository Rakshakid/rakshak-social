# Rakshak ID: launch marketing plan (5 Oct to 16 Dec 2026)

Owner and only approver: Col Arvind Vannur, Veteran (founder). Write his name exactly as "Col Arvind Vannur, Veteran", never "(Retd)".
Goal: grow pre-registrations on https://rakshakid.com before the app launches on Vijay Diwas, 16 December 2026, and tie the phrase "Culture of Gratitude" to Rakshak ID.

## What Rakshak ID is
One app for India's Armed Forces family: Veterans (Army, Navy, Air Force, Assam Rifles, DSC, Coast Guard, CAPF), Veer Naris, Rakshak Naris (spouses/mothers), Agniveers and their families (one Rakshak Number with a verified card for each family member).
Three things it gives: Privileges (discounts and offers), Brotherhood (verified community), Opportunity (alumni network and veteran businesses).
Lines: "You stood for Bharat. Now Bharat stands with you." / "आप भारत के लिए खड़े रहे। अब भारत आपके साथ खड़ा है।" / "वर्दी की शान, हर जगह सम्मान।" / "Culture of Gratitude".
Registering is free. Early members get extra privileges at launch. Verification happens in the app; documents are deleted within 72 hours. Not affiliated with the Ministry of Defence.

## Culture of Gratitude (the spine of the campaign), six parts
1 The World: other nations thank veterans every day; Bharat does it a few days a year. (Week 1, done)
2 The Why: the thank-you must last his whole life; the whole family serves. (Week 1, done)
3 The How: one verified identity turns silent respect into visible action: a discount, a priority queue, a seat, an opened door.
4 The Where: at every shop, school, hospital and boardroom.
5 The Change: Gen Z and Gen Alpha are watching how Bharat treats its soldiers. Let them inherit a nation that remembers. Teaching gratitude to the next generation is the key message.
6 The Goal: a soldier must never fade away; he walks as a role model, earning respect in service, commanding it for life.

## Guardrails (do not break)
- Community size: always "2.5 crore". Never 1 crore or 3 crore.
- No brand names or logos of partners until the founder confirms a signed deal.
- No promise of help or outcomes. Rakshak is a conveyor/networking platform: say "your post reaches thousands of verified Rakshaks", never "your people will turn up / we will help".
- No ranks anywhere. No AI-generated soldiers or faces. No MoD or service logos.
- Never "Founding Member", "first 1,00,000" or "Free for Life". Use "Register early. Early members get extra privileges at launch."
- English and Hindi on every post. Instagram captions cannot hold clickable links: say "link in bio". Facebook/YouTube captions use https://rakshakid.com.
- Keep wording plain and dignified, especially for Veer Nari content.
