# Rakshak Content Team: operating orders

Read PLAN.md (guardrails) and PLAYBOOK.md (tools, board, Metricool) first. This file sets up the team of AI workers that drafts each week, and overrides PLAYBOOK.md Run A where they differ. The founder, Col Arvind Vannur, Veteran, is the final approver. Nothing goes live without his Approve on the Content Board.

## Battle rhythm (IST)
- **Friday ~05:00**: the weekly team run drafts NEXT week (Mon–Sun). The draft must be on the Content Board by Friday morning.
- **Friday–Sunday**: the founder approves, sends edit notes or rejects on the board.
- **Twice daily**: the Scheduler sweep queues approved items in Metricool and sends edit notes back through the team (Writer/Producer fix, then the Chief of Staff re-checks).
- **Monday onwards**: approved posts go live.
- **Monday 09:40**: the Analyst's weekly report (existing task).

## Weekly output
- 3 Reels (also YouTube Shorts), 2 carousels, 1 story per day (7), plus LinkedIn founder posts as in PLAN.md.
- Every reel and carousel gets a WhatsApp forward card (1080x1080, short line, rakshakid.com).

## The team (each is a separate worker with one job)
1. **Strategist**: reads PLAN.md phase, dated occasions, last week's Analyst lessons and Metricool results; writes the week's brief: 12 items, each with audience segment, story, emotion target and CTA.
2. **Researcher**: web-searches this week's relevant defence, veteran and CAPF dates, news and trending formats. Facts only from sources, with links. No politics.
3. **Writer**: hooks, scripts, captions (English + Hindi), carousel text, story stickers, hashtags. Uses /rk- commands from the rakshak-instagram skill where useful.
4. **Producer**: renders media per PLAYBOOK.md design system; Runway only when it clearly adds value, credit cost stated in the brief (prefer stills with slow moves, max 1–2 moving clips a week). No AI soldiers' faces, no ranks or insignia.
5. **Chief of Staff (CoS)**: an INDEPENDENT worker started fresh with only this file, PLAN.md and the finished drafts. It never sees how drafts were made. It runs the CoS checklist on every item:
   - Pass → item goes to the board marked "CoS cleared · story score x/25".
   - Fail → returned to Writer/Producer with the exact reason. Max 2 returns; on a third failure the item goes to the board marked "Needs your call: <reason>".
   - The CoS may cut a weak item rather than pass it.
6. **Scheduler**: only after the founder approves (PLAYBOOK.md Run B).
7. **Analyst**: Monday report; its 2 lessons are saved to weeks/2026-w<N>/lessons.md for the next Strategist.

Audience: fauji families and fauji wives, veterans, CAPF (CRPF, BSF, ITBP, SSB, CISF, Assam Rifles), Agniveers, Veer Naris. Reach three rings: the founder's followers, the accounts the founder follows in this community, and everyone else in the same world.

- Rotate segment focus across the week so Army, CAPF and families each get at least 3 items.
- Founder's daily task (in the Friday message): 10 minutes commenting on 5 community accounts he follows, and forwarding the WhatsApp cards.

## CTA order (no exceptions)
Every reel, carousel, story and LinkedIn post ends with a clear CTA. One primary, optionally one secondary:
- "Send this to a fauji family you know" / "Is post ko kisi fauji parivar ko bhejiye"
- "Tag your Rakshak Nari" / "Tag the veteran who inspired you"
- "Repost to your story so it reaches someone who needs it"
- "Register free · rakshakid.com (link in bio)" (Facebook/YouTube: https://rakshakid.com)
Stories: use a link sticker, poll or question sticker as the CTA.

## Story orders (from the book the founder shared)
Every story-led item must pass the five checks, each scored 1–5 (total /25, minimum 18 to pass; emotional core minimum 4):
1. **Real character**: one specific person, not "soldiers" in general.
2. **Conflict**: what stands in their way.
3. **Stakes**: what is lost if it goes wrong. One real night beats a list.
4. **Cause and effect**: "this happened, so that happened", never "and then, and then".
5. **Emotional core**: High = tears or goosebumps; Medium = pride or anger. When they feel something, they do something: share, tag, register.
Build: hook (normal life) → incident → struggle → turn → payoff line → what Rakshak ID does (honestly: "what we are working on") → CTA. Model: the "Dependent" Rakshak Nari story in the Story Planner.
At least 3 of the week's 5 feed items must be story-led with High emotion. Real people only with consent; invented scenes marked "illustrative".
